00  · About CTO

Translating awareness into action

Community Translation Organisation (CTO) is a non-profit organisation in Cambodia. CTO helps poor rural communities by giving people the skills and tools they need to build a better life.

Many rural families in Cambodia do not have clean water, good schools, or electricity at home. CTO works directly in these areas.

CTO builds community groups, such as savings groups, farmer groups, and women-led groups. These groups speak up for their members and protect the most vulnerable people, including women and ethnic minorities.

CTO believes that when people get the right knowledge and tools, they can change their own lives. Turning that knowledge into real action is what the name "Community Translation" means.

01  · The problem

Your website is holding you back

CTO has 25 years of work with big global partners, including the UN and Oxfam. But right now, the website is making it harder to find new donors and partners.

  • Security risk. The domain and email accounts are controlled by a former staff member in New York. CTO does not have access to its own online accounts.
  • Trust gap. The website has broken links and old content. This makes CTO look inactive to potential donors.
  • Hard to give. There is no simple way for an international donor to send money or get in touch.
02  · What needs to change

Four things the current site gets wrong

Beyond the security issues, the website content itself needs work. Here is what we will fix.

  • Typos and errors. The current site has spelling mistakes and grammar errors throughout. This makes CTO look unprofessional to donors and partner organisations. We will review and correct all text on the site.
  • Old messaging. The writing does not clearly explain what CTO does or why it matters. We will rewrite the key pages with simple, direct language that speaks to donors, partners, and volunteers.
  • Donation information is unclear. Visitors who want to give money have no easy way to find out how. Bank details are missing or hard to find, and there is no clear explanation of how donations are used. We will create a dedicated section with all the information a donor needs.
  • No payment gateway. There is no online payment option for international donors. We will set up a simple, trusted payment link so anyone in the world can donate in a few clicks.
03  · The solution

Four steps to fix this

Phase I

Get your accounts back

Reclaim the domain and email accounts from the former staff member. Set up a secure login system that belongs to CTO, not to one person.

Phase II

Build a new website

A clean, modern one-page site with CTO's mission, history, and team. Impact data shown clearly. WhatsApp and Telegram links for easy contact.

Phase III

Make it easy to donate

A clear section for local QR code payments and international bank transfers. Simple language to explain how donations are used.

Phase IV

Italian outreach page

A separate page at /italiano for Italian donors and Italians living abroad.
