Section 3.0

The Used Import Pipeline: How Toyota & Ford Dominate

Understanding the thirty-year pipeline that established competitor dominance and how Mazda dismantles it.

Stage 1: The Thirty-Year Used Fleet Pipeline

From 1993 to 2018, over 80% of vehicle imports were used US auction models, predominantly Camrys, Corollas, and Priuses. This created a massive national fleet where every provincial mechanic learned to service Toyota systems using widely available scrap parts.

Stage 2: The Self-Sustaining Resale Belief

Because mechanics were universal, buyers treated used Toyotas as liquid financial reserves that could be resold within 48 hours. Toyota's modern market share was built by used fleet familiarity rather than product superiority.

Stage 3: The Mazda Solution

We cannot quickly replicate thirty years of grey imports. Instead, we remove customer hesitation with written Buy-Back Certificates at 36 months, certified independent workshops via the Automotive Centre of Excellence Cambodia (ACE-C), and an active trade-in program for aging Toyotas.

Partnering with ACE-C enables Mazda to certify 25 independent provincial workshops in year one. We supply diagnostic scanners, genuine parts lines, and official branding. This replicates provincial service coverage at a fraction of the cost of building full franchised dealerships.
