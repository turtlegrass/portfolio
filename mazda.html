Section 3.0

The Used Import Pipeline: How Competitors Dominate

How thirty years of used car imports built Toyota and Ford's position, and the strategy to challenge it[cite: 1].

1. The Thirty-Year Used Fleet Inflow

Between 1993 and 2018, more than 80% of vehicle imports were used US auction inventory, mainly Camrys and Corollas. This established a vast fleet where mechanics in every province learned to service Toyota vehicles using inexpensive salvage parts[cite: 1].

2. The Resulting Resale Belief

Because replacement parts were readily available, buyers came to view used Toyotas as liquid financial reserves that could be sold within 48 hours[cite: 1]. Competitor dominance stems from this service availability rather than superior modern design[cite: 1].

3. The Mazda Solution

Rather than attempting to rebuild thirty years of import volume, Mazda provides 36-month Guaranteed Buy-Back Certificates in Khmer[cite: 1]. We back this with 25 certified independent repair shops via the Automotive Centre of Excellence Cambodia (ACE-C), alongside trade-in incentives for older Toyotas[cite: 1].
