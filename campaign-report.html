Key Takeaway

In July 2025, $210.84 in ad spend generated 85 inbox leads for F & S Daycare with an average cost of just $2.48 per lead. All leads delivered were inbox conversations; in-person follow-ups and enrollments were handled separately by the school team.

The top two campaigns (Top Perfomaing ads to be analyzed and scaled) — New Engagement ($1.82/lead) and the Mindfulness post ($1.99/lead) together drove 53% of all leads at the lowest cost per conversation. The Mindfulness post also achieved the highest reach of the campaign at 32,265 unique people for only $31.82.

Scope: Paid inbox leads only · Attribution: 7-day click / 1-day view · Platform: Facebook Ads F & S Daycare
