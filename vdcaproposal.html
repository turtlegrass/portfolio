Project Proposal

New Website & Outreach Plan

Volunteer Development Children's Association (VDCA)  ·  Prepared by Rachel Pilloff Consulting

00  · About VDCA

Free education for children in Siem Reap

VDCA has been running free schools in Cambodia since 2004. It was founded by two Buddhist monks who saw children in Siem Reap with no path forward and decided to build one. What started in two simple classrooms grew into a full school campus, funded entirely by donations from people around the world.

The organisation became an official NGO in 2007. A few years later, it opened a second school in Anlung Pi Village, a community about 25km from the city that had become home to Siem Reap's rubbish dump. Children there needed support, and VDCA showed up.

People from many walks of life support VDCA. Some give once. Some give regularly. Some come to volunteer. All of it goes directly to the children.

Mission

Teach children to take responsibility and make good decisions. Support teachers with training and fair pay. Give international volunteers a way to contribute. Build a school that Cambodians own and run for the long term.

01  · The goals

Three things this project needs to do

VDCA has a strong story and a real track record. The challenge is making that visible and easy to act on for people around the world.

Volunteers

Bring in new local and international volunteers with a clear, welcoming sign-up process and honest information about what to expect.

Donors

Make it easy for anyone in the world to give money online. Show exactly where donations go and why they matter.

International interest

Reconnect with past supporters and reach new audiences through fresh content and a site that works on any device.

New website

Replace the current slow site with a fast, modern, mobile-friendly one that is easy to update and easy to use.

02  · What is wrong now

What the current site gets wrong

The existing website has good content buried inside a slow, outdated build. These are the main problems.

  • Slow loading. The site takes too long to open on mobile. Most international visitors leave a slow site in the first few seconds.
  • Not mobile-friendly. The layout breaks on phones and tablets. Most visitors today browse on a phone.
  • Typos and errors. The current text has spelling mistakes and grammar problems. This makes the organisation look less professional to new donors and volunteers.
  • Old messaging. The writing does not clearly explain what VDCA does today or how to get involved. It needs a full rewrite in plain, welcoming language.
  • Hard to volunteer. The volunteer page does not give enough practical information. Interested people do not know what is needed, how long to come, or what to expect.
03  · The solution

What we will build

Instead of patching the old site, we will build a new one from scratch. It will be fast, simple, and designed to turn visitors into supporters.

Website

New responsive site

A fast, mobile-friendly website that works well on any screen. Clean layout with clear sections for volunteers, donors, and supporters. Easy to read and easy to navigate.

Content

Fresh writing throughout

All text rewritten in plain, correct English at a reading level that works for international visitors. Typos fixed. Messaging updated to reflect what VDCA does now.

Volunteers

Clear volunteer page

A dedicated page explaining how to volunteer, what to expect, how long to come, and how to apply. A simple contact form or WhatsApp link for quick replies.

Donations

Online payment setup

Keep the existing donation setup, which works well, and carry it into the new site. Make sure the donate button is visible and prominent on every section of the page.

Outreach

International-facing content

A section that speaks directly to international supporters: the school's story, photos, impact, and ways to stay connected. Social media links made visible and easy to find.
