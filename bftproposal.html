Executive Summary

What this proposal covers

Vision

"To give BFT a digital presence that reflects 20 years of extraordinary impact and makes it easier than ever for supporters around the world to find them, trust them, and give."

BFT has operated for 20 years and reached over 100,000 people across rural Cambodia. The digital infrastructure has not kept pace. This proposal addresses three specific gaps with three focused solutions, scoped to be affordable and genuinely useful to a lean team.

ComponentWhat it delivers

Project Background

The story so far

Build Your Future Today Center has been transforming lives in rural Cambodia since 2006. Over two decades, BFT has built 99 primary school classrooms, reached over 100,000 people through education, health, and capacity-building programs, and grown a global network of donors and partners across Australia, the USA, the UK, Europe, Hong Kong, Singapore, and beyond. This proposal is about giving that legacy the digital infrastructure to match.

OpportunityWhy it matters
A story worth telling beautifully Two decades of transformation and a global support network. A well-built website puts that front and centre for every new visitor who arrives already interested in BFT's work.
A giving pathway that converts Supporters who are ready to give deserve a smooth, trustworthy experience. Multiple payment options and QR codes make giving easy from anywhere in the world.
A unified view of supporters BFT's global donor community is one of its greatest assets. A CRM brings all supporter data together so the organisation can nurture relationships and grow its funding base with confidence.
Fresh branding, finally live BFT's new logo and visual identity are ready. This proposal applies them across every page so the public face of the organisation reflects the same quality as its programs on the ground.
